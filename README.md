# Financial Statement and Ratio Analysis Report
## Reliance Industries Limited — FY 2025–26

This project involves analyzing the **three** main **Financial Statements** — **Profit and Loss Statement (Income Statement)**, **Balance Sheet** and the **Cash Flow Statement** of Reliance Industries Ltd. FY 2025-2026.<br/>

As a data analyst, it is essential to understand basics of accounting to be able to make sense of these numbers and to accurately make decisions on financial health of a company.<br/>

For this project, the **Consolidated Financial Statements** have been looked into, along with [Management Discussion & Analysis Report (MD&A)](https://www.ril.com/ar2025-26/financial-performance-and-review.html) obtained from the [Reliance Industries Ltd. Official Website](https://www.ril.com/ar2025-26/index.html).<br/>

### Executive Summary
The financial analysis of FY 2025–26 compared with FY 2024–25 presents a broadly positive picture of profitability and shareholder returns, accompanied by a modest softening in operating efficiency and liquidity. Based on the simplified financial statements and ratios calculated, revenue increased from ₹9,80,136 crore to ₹10,75,675 crore, while Profit After Tax (PAT) increased from ₹80,787 crore to ₹95,610 crore. This supported improvement in the Profit Margin, ROA and ROE.<br/>

At the same time, Debtors Turnover, Inventory Turnover and Asset Turnover moderated, indicating that the growth in the asset base and working-capital requirements was somewhat faster than the corresponding improvement in operating activity. The Debt-Equity ratio remained broadly stable, while the Interest Coverage measure remained strong despite a small decline.<br/>

Management's FY 2025–26 commentary describes the Group as having delivered a strong financial performance, supported by its diversified portfolio, robust Indian market demand, internal cash-flow generation and disciplined capital allocation. Management also highlighted strong liquidity management, sufficient undrawn credit lines and prudent leverage management.<br/>

**Overall analyst view:** The two-year trend suggests a company that improved its earnings quality and returns while continuing to invest significantly for future growth. The main areas deserving attention are working-capital efficiency, asset utilisation and the maintenance of comfortable short-term liquidity as the business expands.<br/>

### Project Process
- Obtaining Financial Statements, MD&A, Director's or Chairman's Report
- Analyzing the Consolidated Financial Statements
- Creating a Simplified version of the Financial Statements for further analysis
- Calculating Financial Ratios for the years
- Creating a Two-year Trend Analysis Report
- Creating Visualizations in Excel for better representation of the numbers
- Correlating the ratios to the MD&A to derive explanations
- Providing insights based on calculated ratio analysis
- Creating a report summarizing the analysis.<br/>
